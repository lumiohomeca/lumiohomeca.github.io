Watch school

Good information
should feel simple.

Power reserve

How long an automatic or manual watch can keep running after it is fully wound. An 80-hour reserve can often carry it through a weekend.

Water resistance

It is not a universal diving permission. Each rating is explained in context so you know what daily life it actually suits.

Case diameter

Numbers need context. The site explains how a size may feel on different wrists rather than declaring a single size “right.”

About Lumio Watches

Independent
by design.

Lumio Watches is a Canadian editorial discovery platform for people who want more context before choosing a watch. We explain what a reference is, where it fits, who sells it and what to verify before buying.

We are not a watch brand, retailer or authorized dealer. When a relevant purchase option exists, it is identified clearly and the transaction takes place on the seller’s own site.
