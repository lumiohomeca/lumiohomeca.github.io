# Lumio Watches — sources & partner shortlist

Status: research only. No affiliate program is integrated and no commercial link may be added until Lumio approves it.

## Editorial source hierarchy

1. **Manufacturer / exact reference page** — primary source for reference, movement, materials, dimensions, water resistance, warranty and official price.
2. **Manufacturer documentation** — manuals, technical PDFs and warranty documents.
3. **Independent specialist coverage** — used for context, fit, historical framing and editorial comparison. Starting references: Fratello and Worn & Wound.
4. **Seller pages** — only for availability, price and terms on the date checked; never the only source for technical claims.

Each product page will show a **last verified date** and a dedicated sources section. A claim that cannot be substantiated remains unpublished.

## Non-negotiable seller labels

- **AUTHORIZED DEALER** — confirmed against the brand’s official dealer locator.
- **MARKETPLACE** — third-party listings; seller identity and returns shown separately.
- **PRE-OWNED** — condition, authenticity process and warranty shown clearly.
- **GREY MARKET** — never called authorized; manufacturer-warranty differences disclosed.

## Rejection criteria

Do not feature a seller if we cannot clearly establish return policy, warranty handling, payment security, seller identity and Canadian delivery/tax implications.
