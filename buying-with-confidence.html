Lumio researches watches and helps readers make sense of them. We do not process payments, hold inventory or decide a seller’s return policy.

What Lumio is

An independent editorial guide. We explain the model, its purpose, its limits and where to verify it before a purchase.

What Lumio is not

We are not a watch brand, retailer, authorized dealer, repairer or marketplace. Any purchase happens directly on the seller’s own site.

Prices

A price is a dated Canadian reference, not a promise. Availability, taxes, shipping, discounting and exchange rates can change.

Commercial links

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. If Lumio earns a commission, it is at no extra cost to the reader and never changes our stated limitations.

Our process

How a watch earns a place here.

  1. Specific reference first.We publish a known model and reference rather than a vague “best watch” recommendation.
  2. Source before claim.Key specifications, Canadian reference pricing and availability are checked against an official maker or reputable seller source.
  3. Context, not hype.Every guide explains who the watch suits, what it does well and what it does not do well.
  4. Clear image labels.Original Lumio photos, generic watchmaking visuals and unverified store sightings are identified separately.
  5. Seller stays accountable.Before buying, readers must review the seller’s price, condition, warranty, delivery and return terms.
Store sightings

When a reference is still unknown.

An original Lumio store photograph may appear before a model is fully identified. In that case it remains editorial only: we do not attach a purchase link, price or invented specification until the exact reference can be verified.

A useful final check

Ask three questions.

Is this the exact reference? Is the seller authorized or clear about condition and warranty? Does this size and purpose actually fit the way I dress and live?

Corrections

Accuracy is part of trust.

If you find a price, reference or specification that needs correction, please contact Lumio through @lumiowatchesca. We welcome source-backed corrections.
