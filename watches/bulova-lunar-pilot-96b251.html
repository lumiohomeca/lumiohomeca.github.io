Why it matters

A story that is more than marketing.

On 2 August 1971, a Bulova chronograph engineered for lunar conditions was worn during Apollo 15. The 96B251 translates that reference into a modern watch with a high-frequency quartz movement, sapphire crystal and practical chronograph layout.

Where it fits

For the curious collector.

This is strongest with casual tailoring, denim, leather and travel clothing. Its NASA-era connection gives a conversation piece without requiring a fragile vintage watch.

Real limits

Heritage means size.

At 45 mm, this is not discreet. The large case and busy tachymeter dial are best for medium and larger wrists, or for buyers who value the historical silhouette over minimalist proportions.

Specifications

What you are getting.

45 mm 316L steel case; black dial; anti-reflective sapphire crystal; proprietary 262 kHz high-performance quartz chronograph; tachymeter; calendar; luminous hands and markers; 50 m water resistance; black leather and black nylon straps; certificate of authenticity.
