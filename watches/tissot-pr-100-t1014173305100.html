Why it matters

Swiss utility in a quiet package.

The PR 100 translates a conventional Swiss chronograph into a cleaner daily watch. Sapphire crystal, a 10.71 mm case and a black bracelet make it more polished than a sports watch without making it fragile.

Where it fits

Office, dinners and travel.

It works with tailoring, darker casual clothing and travel days where a date and timing function are useful. The monochrome finish makes it a more discreet option than a bright-dial chronograph.

Real limits

Quartz, not mechanical theatre.

The PowerDrive quartz movement offers accuracy and low maintenance, but it does not give the hand-wound ritual or visible mechanical character of an automatic. Its 41 mm size also favours medium and larger wrists.

Specifications

What you are getting.

41 mm black-PVD 316L stainless-steel case and bracelet; sapphire crystal; Swiss PowerDrive quartz chronograph; central 60-second chronograph hand; 30-minute and 1/10-second counters; ADD and SPLIT functions; date; Super-LumiNova hands; jewellery clasp; 100 m water resistance; 2-year warranty.
