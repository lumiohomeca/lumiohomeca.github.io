Why it matters

Durability with a point of view.

The GM‑2100 puts a forged stainless-steel bezel over G-SHOCK’s famously tough construction. The octagonal profile and hairline metal finish give it a visual identity that feels intentional rather than merely sporty.

Where it fits

For a sharper casual uniform.

It works for travel, city weekends, creative workplaces and casual dinners. Pair it with black, navy, grey or denim; it has enough texture to hold its own, but it is not trying to behave like a formal dress watch.

Real limits

Metal details, not full metal.

The bezel is stainless steel, but the case and band are resin. That keeps it light and practical, but it does not have the all-metal bracelet feel of the more expensive G-SHOCK full-metal lines.
