Why it matters

A practical first automatic.

The 98B471 gives a new buyer more substance than a fashion watch: an automatic movement, an exhibition back, sapphire crystal and a unidirectional timing bezel. The green dial keeps it distinct without becoming difficult to wear.

Where it fits

Travel, weekends and daily rotation.

The 41 mm case and H-link bracelet make it a versatile option for casual offices, city walks, holiday travel and water-adjacent days. It is sportier than a dress watch, but refined enough to sit under a relaxed jacket.

Real limits

It is not a minimalist piece.

The green-and-black bezel and gold-tone details make a clear visual statement. Automatic movements also need wrist time or manual winding after they stop, unlike quartz or Eco-Drive alternatives.

Specifications

What you are getting.

41 mm stainless-steel case; green sunray dial; stainless-steel H-link bracelet; anti-reflective sapphire crystal; automatic calibre 8215 with 42-hour reserve; exhibition caseback; screw-down crown; unidirectional aluminium bezel; calendar; luminous hands and markers; and 200 m water resistance.
