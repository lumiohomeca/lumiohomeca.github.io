Why it matters

A dress watch that keeps working.

The Corso gives a more formal direction to an Eco‑Drive chronograph: a dark blue dial, steel bracelet and sapphire crystal, without the maintenance routine of a mechanical watch or a regular battery change.

Where it fits

Work, events, and smart evenings.

This is for tailored casual dressing, office wear, dinners and occasions where a resin sports watch would feel too relaxed. The blue dial adds depth without forcing a loud colour story.

Real limits

Polished, but not small.

At 42 mm, it has a noticeable presence. It will suit medium and larger wrists best, or anyone who likes a visible chronograph. Its dressy character is stronger than its tool-watch credentials.
