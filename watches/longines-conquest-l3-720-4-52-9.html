Why it matters

A name with history.

Longines registered Conquest in 1954, its first watch collection to carry a protected name. This current 38 mm model turns that sports-elegant idea into a versatile daily watch.

The one trait to know

Real all-day versatility.

The automatic movement offers up to 72 hours of power reserve and a silicon balance spring. A screw-in crown, sapphire crystal and 10 bar water resistance make it more carefree than a delicate dress watch.

Where it fits

Meetings to weekends.

Ideal for work, business travel, restaurant dinners and smart-casual events. Its clean 38 mm case can suit many wrists; the rubber strap makes this version feel sportier than a bracelet or leather version.

Real limits

Not a formal icon.

It is practical and polished, but not the artistic dress-watch statement of a Cartier Tank and not a dedicated diver. The black rubber strap may feel too casual for a wedding or very formal tailoring.

Specifications

What you are getting.

38 mm stainless-steel case; black sunray dial; automatic movement at 25,200 vibrations/hour; date; up to 72-hour reserve; monocrystalline silicon balance spring; sapphire crystal with anti-reflective coating; screw-in crown; 10 bar water resistance; rubber strap with micro-adjust clasp.

Fair alternative

Choose Hamilton instead if…

You value military simplicity, a slimmer hand-wound watch and a lower price. Choose the Conquest if you want more water resistance, automatic winding and a more polished one-watch-for-most-things character.
