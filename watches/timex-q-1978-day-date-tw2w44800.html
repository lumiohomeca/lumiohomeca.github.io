Why it matters

Seventies without the fuss.

The Q Timex takes the design language of the late 1970s — blue sunray dial, gold-tone details and a domed crystal — and pairs it with a simple quartz movement. It is a style-first piece that remains easy to live with.

Where it fits

Small, expressive, easy.

The 37.5 mm case is a good option for smaller wrists or anyone tired of oversized watches. It fits casual offices, dinners, travel and a simple knit, jacket or open-collar shirt.

Real limits

Style over sport.

The leather strap, acrylic crystal and 30 m water resistance make this a city watch, not a swimming or outdoor-tool watch. Acrylic can scratch, although it has the warm look that makes this kind of reissue feel convincing.

Specifications

What you are getting.

37.5 mm polished stainless-steel case; blue sunray dial; gold-tone hands and markers; analog quartz movement; day/date at 3 o'clock; domed acrylic crystal; black leather strap; 30 m water resistance.
