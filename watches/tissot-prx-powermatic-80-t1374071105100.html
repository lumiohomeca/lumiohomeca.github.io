Why it matters

1978, made current.

The PRX is rooted in Tissot’s 1978 integrated-bracelet sports watches. The modern Powermatic version keeps that geometric profile but adds a mechanical movement and patterned dial.

Where it fits

Modern, clean, versatile.

Good for a modern office, dinners, smart-casual events and travel. The black dial is the most understated route into the collection.

Real limits

Not a tool watch.

The bracelet and polished surfaces make it refined, but less casual and less rugged than the Hamilton or Seiko. Its visual presence may not suit every small wrist.

Specifications

What you are getting.

Swiss Powermatic 80 automatic movement; 40 mm steel case and integrated bracelet; sapphire crystal; exhibition back; date; approximately 80-hour reserve; 10 bar water resistance.
