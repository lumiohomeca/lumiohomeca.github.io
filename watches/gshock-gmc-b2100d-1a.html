Why it matters

The G-SHOCK, dressed up.

The GMC-B2100D takes the octagonal 2100 language and applies it to a fully analog chronograph. Casio uses a stainless-steel case, bezel and bracelet, while resin buffering components preserve the shock-resistant structure.

Where it fits

Travel, city, movement.

This is for someone who wants one robust watch that can still work with a dark knit, clean shirt, overshirt or jacket. It is substantial, so it makes more sense as a deliberate presence than an invisible everyday piece.

Real limits

Big and technical.

At 46.3 mm wide and 171 g, this is not the quietest choice for a small wrist or formal black-tie setting. The value comes from its resilience, solar charging and rich case finishing rather than traditional mechanical watchmaking.

Specifications

What you are getting.

51.3 × 46.3 × 12.4 mm stainless-steel case; screw-lock crown; 200 m water resistance; Tough Solar charging; Bluetooth smartphone link; dual time; chronograph; daily alarm; LED light; Made in Japan.
