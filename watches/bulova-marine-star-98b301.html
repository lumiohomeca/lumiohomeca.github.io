Why it matters

Sport presence with a dressy finish.

The Marine Star uses a functional chronograph and rotating bezel, then adds a blue dial and rose-gold-tone detailing for a more expressive look. It is not quiet, but it gives casual and resort dressing a deliberate focal point.

Where it fits

Weekends, travel and water-adjacent days.

Wear it with a polo, open-collar shirt, knitwear or a relaxed jacket. Its 200 m rating makes it a more credible companion for swimming than a typical dress chronograph, while the bracelet keeps the finish sharp for evenings.

Real limits

Big wrist presence.

The 45 mm case, two-tone finish and busy dial make this a statement watch. It will suit larger wrists and buyers who want their watch to be noticed; minimalist dress-watch shoppers should look elsewhere.

Specifications

What you are getting.

45 mm two-tone stainless-steel case; blue dial with rose-gold-tone luminous hands and markers; stainless-steel bracelet; domed mineral crystal; quartz calibre 6S20; six-hand 1/20-second chronograph measuring up to 60 minutes; calendar; small seconds; rotating bezel; and 200 m water resistance.
