Lumio editorial selection · October 2026

Five places
to start well.

Not a universal ranking. These are five specific watches for five genuinely different lives, selected for context, capability and a clear reason to exist.

Every pick links to a detailed Lumio guide. Prices are Canadian references and must be checked with the seller before purchase.

01
Best first automatic · CAD 500–700

Citizen TSUYOSA
NJ0150-56E

The most balanced starting point for someone who wants their first mechanical watch to feel intentional, not overly precious. Its automatic movement, sapphire crystal, integrated bracelet and versatile black dial cover workdays, dinners and weekends without asking the owner to build a whole collection first.
