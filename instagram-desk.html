Every Lumio store photograph now has a distinct editorial route and one fixed posting slot. Current watches explain real retail context. Archive references explain what to verify before assigning a market price. Unconfirmed sightings stay transparent about what is—and is not—known.

Three content lanes.

New & current: reference, useful specification and current Canadian price context.

Archive & pre-owned: design history, exact reference and value factors—never a guessed resale number.

Store sighting: visual analysis and verification process, without a purchase claim.

Publishing queue: current references first, archive next, research last. Every slot locks one original photo to one model.
